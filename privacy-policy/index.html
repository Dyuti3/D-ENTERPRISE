Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 6th November 2026

D ENTERPRISE ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website, services, and educational programmes.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we may share it, and how you can contact us regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our website, register for a course, make a purchase, or contact us, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal information: Name, email address, phone number, and other details you voluntarily provide.
  • Transaction information: Course purchased, payment status, transaction reference, and related purchase details.
  • Account information: Login credentials and information required to provide access to purchased courses, if accounts are offered.
  • Communication information: Messages, enquiries, feedback, and support requests you send us.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and website usage data, where collected through cookies or analytics tools.

We collect only the information reasonably required to operate our website and provide our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Process course registrations and online payments.
  • Provide access to purchased educational content.
  • Respond to enquiries and provide customer support.
  • Send important updates about courses, purchases, or services.
  • Improve our website, services, and user experience.
  • Maintain transaction records and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We will not use your personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or your consent, where required.

3. Online Payments

Payments made through our website may be processed by third-party payment service providers.

D ENTERPRISE does not intend to collect or store complete debit card, credit card, UPI PIN, or banking passwords on its own servers. Payment information is handled by the relevant payment provider according to its own privacy and security practices.

Please review the payment provider's privacy policy before completing a transaction.

4. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Support essential website functionality.
  • Remember user preferences.
  • Understand website traffic and performance.
  • Improve website usability.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work properly if essential cookies are disabled.

If we use third-party analytics or advertising tools, their respective privacy policies may also apply.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share relevant information with trusted third parties when necessary to operate our services, including:

  • Payment gateway providers.
  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Email, communication, or customer-support services.
  • Analytics and security service providers.
  • Government authorities or law-enforcement agencies when required by applicable law.

Such sharing will be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose or legal requirement.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no online platform or electronic transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also take reasonable precautions to protect their account credentials and devices.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, fulfil the purposes described in this policy, resolve disputes, maintain business records, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable retention requirements.
