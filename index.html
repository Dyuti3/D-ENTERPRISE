Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before enrolling with Denterprise.

Do you give stock tips or recommendations? +
Absolutely not. Denterprise is a pure education platform. We teach you how to analyse markets, read charts, and build your own strategy. We never recommend specific stocks or give buy/sell calls. All investment decisions are entirely your own.
Who is this suitable for? +
Our courses are designed for anyone — complete beginners with zero market knowledge to intermediate traders looking to sharpen their skills. Each course clearly states its level so you can pick the right starting point.
Is access really lifetime? +
Yes. Once enrolled, you get lifetime access to all course materials including any future updates. Learn at your own pace — no deadlines, no expiry.
What language are the courses in? +
Our courses are taught in Hindi and English, making them accessible across India. Study materials are provided in both languages.
How is Elite Mentorship different? +
Elite Mentorship gives you direct one-on-one sessions, a personalised review of your own strategy, and priority WhatsApp support. It's for those who want a hands-on, guided learning experience.
How do I enroll and pay? +
Fill out the enrolment form below — our team will reach out within 24 hours with payment details and onboarding steps. You can also message us directly on WhatsApp using the green button on this page.
Is there a refund policy? +
We offer a 7-day review period. If you feel the course isn't the right fit within the first 7 days, contact us and we'll sort it out. Your satisfaction comes first.
